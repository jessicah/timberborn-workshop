# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I get beavers to spawn?

If you're encountering a problem on getting beavers to spawn then you
must be insisting on using the IronTeeth housing instead of the
Fokltail housing. No building has been modified by this mod. If it did
then I could not do speedy updates when a patch/update breaks the
faction. Lodges and pods spawn beavers because they have a spawn rate,
barracks do not because they never had one to begin with.

## Could you add X from Y mod?

The author of said mod is responsible for adding those tools to
GreedyBuilder's tool list, not me. If I do it my end then I would not
be able to quickly update GreedyBuilders whenever a patch/update breaks
it because of the need to wait for the mod you wanted me to support.

All the author has to do in general is create a copy of their Folktail
prefab group and rename a couple of things from "Folktails" to
"GreedyBuilders" (excluding the tool names of course). If any of their
IronTeeth variants has more functionality (for example the Industrial
Lumbermill of IronTeeth has more than the Lumbermill of the Folktails
so it was chosen instead) then that building should replace the
Folktails variant in the GreedyBuilders prefab group they make.

## Why did you not include every building from each faction?

Not a FAQ at the moment but I wouldn't be surprised if it does become
one so I'll address it here. The game shares names for some buildings
which causes TB to crash if both are defined to be included in the same
faction. For this reason I chose to prioritise functionality over looks.
When functionality was the same (such as in the forester case) I chose
to focus on the looks of the Folktails faction since I like their
aesthetics more than IronTeeth's.

## Why do the bot's walk below ziplines instead of riding them?

I did try setting the required bot to the Folktail variant but that
crashed the game. I could add a custom bot for the faction but that
would remove the ability to quickly update the mod to support the
newest Timberborn update and/or patch so glitchy bot ziplining is
what you get ¯\_(ツ)_/¯

That said at the time of writing this FAQ file I have been considering
a separate mod to address that issue specifically. By doing it
separatly it means I can keep GreedyBuilders easy to update quickly
and if the fix breaks then it's not a big deal, just disable the fix
and carry on :D

## I thought you did X?

This type of question typically comes from steam workshop users where
steam has failed to auto-update their copy of the mod. A simple fix is
to just un-subscribe then re-subscribe. A better way to do it is to go
into Timberborn's properties dialouge that steam offers, find the
"Verify all files" that it has (may have mis-remembered the exact text)
and click that. That button will update not just GreedyBuilders but
any other Timbeborn workshop mod steam had thus-far failed to update.

For players using mod.io mods the mod manager is the recommended way
to manage mods, even if it does sometimes accidently wipe the mod's
contents altogether. In that particular case it's sufficient to just
delete the mod folder and re-download it.

## How do I unlock the faction?

It used to be the case GreedyBuilders required 45 well being from the
IronTeeth faction to be achieved. At the time I wanted to encourage
playing both vanilla factions prior to playing this merger of the 2.
Then for one reason or another I removed it temporarily and never got
round to restoring the requirement.

I've since decided to leave it as auto unlocked however players that
had the mod installed prior to this change will still have the boolean
stored in `<My Documents>/Timberborn/PlayerData/player.data/data.json`
set to false. There's a few ways to resolve this:

A. The easiest way is to enter dev mode (`[shift]+[alt]+[z]`) in the
   main menu then enter the settings menu and click
   "Unlock All Factions". This will however effect other locked
   factions which you may want to unlock normally, if so try one of the
   next 2.
B. Just enter a session with any unlocked faction for the game to
   trigger the unlock.
C. This one is not recommended, in fact the devs have explcitly
   recommended against this sort of thing after I queried the saftey
   of it, then again I'm not your mom so you do you ¯\_(ツ)_/¯.
   1. Rename `player.data` to `player.zip`
   2. Extract the `data.json` from it
   3. Edit the `data.json` to say GreedyBuilders is unlocked
   4. Overwrite the `player.zip`'s `data.json` with the newly edited
      version
   5. Rename the `player.zip` back to `player.data`
   Linux users can skip the renaming steps since linux apps tend to
   ignore the file extension in favour of the detected mimetype (done
   for security I think).
