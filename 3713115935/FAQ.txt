# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I get beavers to spawn?

No building has been modified by this mod. If the vanilla faction needs
<X> building to spawn beavers then you can either use the same building
or another faction's building to spawn beavers. The game just selects a
random texture from the list to apply to them so you could have IT born
in a FT lodge or create FT in IT pods.

## I thought you did X?

This type of question typically comes from steam workshop users where
steam has failed to auto-update their copy of the mod. A simple fix is
to just un-subscribe then re-subscribe. A better way to do it is to go
into Timberborn's properties dialouge that steam offers, find the
"Verify all files" that it has (may have mis-remembered the exact text)
and click that. That button will update not just GreedyBuilders but
any other Timbeborn workshop mod steam had thus-far failed to update.

For players using mod.io mods the mod manager is the recommended way
to manage mods, even if it does sometimes accidently wipe the mod's
contents altogether. In that particular case it's sufficient to just
delete the mod folder and re-download it
